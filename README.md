# Sarasota Paint Pro

Build a professional, trustworthy website for Tad Dailey Painting, LLC, a residential and light-commercial painting company based in Sarasota, Florida.

Services to feature:

Interior painting

Exterior painting

Wood staining

Epoxy flooring/coatings

Cabinet painting/refinishing

Pressure washing

Key business details:

15+ years of experience — should be prominent in the About section and reinforced elsewhere (hero tagline, trust badges, etc.)

Family-owned/local Sarasota business feel

No online quotes or price calculators — all estimates are done via phone call and an in-person visit, so the primary call-to-action throughout the site should be "Call for a Free Estimate" (with the phone number highly visible in the header, hero section, and footer — not buried in a contact form)

Service area: Sarasota, FL and surrounding areas (mention nearby towns if relevant)

Pages/sections needed:

Home — hero image/banner, tagline emphasizing quality + experience, services overview with icons, prominent "Call Now" CTA, brief trust signals (years in business, licensed/insured if applicable, reviews)

Services — dedicated section or subpages for each service listed above, with a short description of what's included

About — story of the business, 15 years of experience, values (craftsmanship, reliability, attention to detail)

Gallery/Portfolio — before/after style layout for painting and epoxy work (use placeholder images for now since I'll need to replace existing low-quality photos with better ones)

Reviews/Testimonials — space for Google reviews or quotes from past customers

Contact — phone number prominent, simple contact form as a secondary option, service area map

Design direction:

Clean, modern, and trustworthy — avoid looking like a generic template

Warm, approachable color palette (not overly corporate) — consider earthy or coastal tones to reflect the Sarasota/Florida setting

Mobile-first, since most visitors will likely be searching on their phone and calling directly

Large, clear typography and generous white space so it reads as professional, not cluttered

Fast-loading, since a slow site will lose local search traffic

Technical notes:

Optimize for local SEO (Sarasota painting contractor, exterior painting Sarasota FL, etc.)

Make the phone number a clickable tel: link on mobile

Keep it simple to update — this is a small business, not a large e-commerce site
